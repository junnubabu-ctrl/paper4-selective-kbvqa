# PhD Progression — Evidence-Based Mapping

## Paper 1 — literature foundation
**Title:** *Advancements in Knowledge-Based Visual Question Answering Using Large Language Models: A Review*.
Role: taxonomy/review of KB-VQA and LLM-assisted methods; establishes gaps in knowledge relevance, visual–knowledge alignment, reasoning, interpretability, computational cost, and hallucination risk.

## Paper 2 — structured reasoning framework
**Title:** *Knowledge-Augmented Visual Question Answering via Multimodal Deep Learning: A Structured Reasoning Framework*.
Role: structured pipeline connecting visual encoding, external knowledge retrieval/injection, prompt/context construction, reasoning, and answer selection/aggregation.

## Paper 3 — adaptive knowledge/fusion/reasoning
**Title:** *KEAF-Net: Knowledge-Enhanced Adaptive Fusion Network for Visual Question Answering with Multi-Hop Graph Reasoning*.
Role: advances the framework through Adaptive Knowledge Filtering (AKF), Heterogeneous Graph Adaptive Fusion (HGAF), and Multi-Hop Semantic Reasoning (MHSR).

**Integrity note:** numerical claims present in existing Paper-3 manuscript copies must not be inherited into Paper 4 unless independently backed by actual experiment logs. Paper 4 treats Paper 3 as conceptual/progression context, not as a source of reusable benchmark numbers.

## Paper 4 — reliability layer
**Working title:** *Evidence Verification and Selective Answering for Knowledge-Based Visual Question Answering with Large Language Models*.
Role: moves downstream from retrieval/fusion/reasoning to answer reliability: evidence provenance, contradiction-aware verification, confidence calibration, risk-controlled selective answering, and abstention.

### Non-duplication rule
Paper 4 must not claim adaptive filtering itself as the central novelty because Paper 3 already occupies that contribution. The Paper-4 novelty must be demonstrated in the reliability layer after evidence is retrieved/filtered.

## Thesis progression
Literature gaps → structured knowledge-augmented reasoning → adaptive fusion/multi-hop reasoning → evidence verification/calibrated selective answering → later adaptive reasoning-depth extension.
